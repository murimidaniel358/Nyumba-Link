About Nyumba Link

We help Kenyan students find accommodation by making it easier to compare prices, locations and facilities.

Our mission

To make finding a safe, affordable student home simple, wherever you're studying.

The problem

Students often search through word of mouth, notice boards and scattered social posts, sometimes from far away. It is hard to compare options or know what is still available.

How it works

Landlords and agents list properties. Students search by campus, budget and facilities, compare, then contact the advertiser directly to arrange a viewing.

Listing accuracy and safety

Each listing shows when availability was last confirmed, and anyone can report a listing. Nyumba Link is a listing platform: unless a listing says otherwise, we have not verified the property or advertiser. Read our safety guidance.
