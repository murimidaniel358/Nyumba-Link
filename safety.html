Safety & scam prevention

You may be searching from far away, so protect your money before you pay.

1. View before payingVisit in person, or ask a trusted person to. Be wary of anyone who refuses a viewing.
2. Confirm who you're dealing withCheck the landlord or agent's identity and that they have authority to let the property.
3. Verify every chargeGet rent, deposit, and extra charges in writing before paying.
4. Resist pressure"Pay now or lose it" is a warning sign.
5. Keep a recordPay by traceable methods and keep receipts and messages.
6. Report suspicious adsUse Report on any listing.

What MyKejaLink does and does not do


  • Listings are published by advertisers. Unless a listing explicitly says otherwise, we have not verified the property or the advertiser.
  • Each listing shows when availability was last confirmed.
  • We review reports and may remove listings or suspend accounts.
  • We never ask students to pay us a booking fee for a property. (Edit to match your policy.)

If you think you've been scammed, report it to us and to the police.
